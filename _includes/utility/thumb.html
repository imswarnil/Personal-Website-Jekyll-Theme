{%- comment -%} A cover, drawn rather than photographed. Used wherever a card, a list or a share image needs a picture and the entry did not provide one. Nothing is fetched and nothing is built: an inline SVG, made from the entry's collection. It carries NO TITLE. The title is already under the card, in real type that wraps, reflows and can be selected — setting it again inside a picture means it is set twice, at a size nobody chose, and clipped the moment the card is narrower than the artwork. What the picture says instead is WHAT KIND of thing this is: the collection's colour, its pattern, and its mark. The file-based twin is tools/thumbs.mjs (`npm run thumbs`), which draws the same picture as a real .svg for the cases that need a URL. Params: item (required) size (kept for callers; the art is the same at every size) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign it = include.item -%} {%- assign label = it.collection | default: "pages" -%} {%- comment -%} The pattern varies by title length — a cheap, stable hash, so the same entry always gets the same art and neighbours rarely match. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign v = it.title | size | modulo: 5 -%} {%- assign uid = it.url | default: it.title | slugify -%}
